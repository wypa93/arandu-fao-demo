Why some report requests could not be completed (per month)
Methodology. Vigor = Sentinel-2 NDVI, leaf greenness = NDRE (B8/B5), leaf water = NDWI (B8/B11), 10–20 m, 14-day cloud-masked composite (Cloud Score+ ≥ 0.60, ≥ 30 % of field clear). When no clear image exists, crop health and water use Sentinel-1 radar (limited confidence) and nitrogen is unavailable. Pineapple thresholds are literature priors, not yet calibrated on Suriname fields. Flood alerts use Sentinel-1 VV < −18 dB.
Privacy. Field-level views require farmers' consent to share with FAO. Aggregates suppress any district / week cell with fewer than 3 farmers. Farmer names are never shown, only pseudonymous IDs.

📏 How we ranked
  • Value to the chain. Does it save pineapples, technician time or money, or produce evidence a funder or buyer asks for?
  • Ready today. Live in the WhatsApp menu, a small addition, or a Phase 2 build.
  • Works through clouds. The Guiana Shield blocks optical images on 27–33 % of days (our 12-month replay). Radar, photos and weather do not care.
  • Can be proven in Phase 1. Maps to an exit condition: ≥ 70 % technician agreement, radar coverage in cloudy weeks, demo vs. traditional baseline.
Our ranking is a starting point. What loses the most pineapples today, and which villages are hardest hit, will move it.
